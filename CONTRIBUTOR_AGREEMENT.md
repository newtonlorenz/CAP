Version 1.0 — prepared for the first public source candidate.

than the rights CAP users receive under the Sustainable Use License.

## 1. Scope and parties

“You” means the individual accepting this agreement or the named legal entity
on whose behalf an authorised representative accepts it. Daniel Graetzer is the
recipient of these grants. `newtonlorenz` is the GitHub account; it does not identify a separate contracting
company here.
“Contribution” means the code,
documentation, tests or assets you intentionally offer for inclusion in CAP in
the pull request identified in your acceptance. It includes your subsequent
changes to that pull request, subject to the confirmation procedure below.

This agreement does not cover unrelated work, a discussion or bug report without
an offered contribution, or material expressly marked “Not a Contribution”.
Each contributor must accept for their own work or have authority to accept for
its owner. A pull request author cannot accept for other authors without that
authority.

## 4. Authority and provenance

You confirm that you have the authority to make these grants. Where an employer,
client or another owner has rights in the Contribution, obtain its authorisation
before accepting and identify the entity you represent. Employment, access to a
repository or a commit author field alone does not establish that authority.

Identify copied or adapted third-party material, its source, licence, required
notices and any known restrictions in the pull request. Do not claim ownership
of that material or grant rights you do not have. Maintainers must review its
terms separately before inclusion. Disclose material AI assistance and any
known source or licence restrictions; generated output is not proof of ownership.
Do not include private documents, personal data or confidential material without
the necessary permission. Notify maintainers if you later discover that your
authority or provenance statement was inaccurate.

## 5. No obligation to support or merge

You do not have to maintain the Contribution. Except for your express authority
and provenance confirmations and obligations imposed by law, it is provided as
is, without a promise of performance or fitness. Daniel Graetzer may decline it.
Acceptance does not promise payment, a maintainer role or inclusion in a release.

## 6. Explicit acceptance for each pull request

Read this version, then post the following statement from your own GitHub account
in the pull request description or a comment, completing the identity fields:

> I have read and accept the CAP Contributor Agreement version 1.0 at
> CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md, agreement revision: [full base commit SHA]. I grant
> Daniel Graetzer the copyright and patent permissions described there, including
> sublicensing and commercial relicensing, for my contributions to this pull
> request. I confirm my authority and have disclosed third-party material and
> known restrictions. Contributor: [name]; acting for: [myself or legal entity].

Opening a pull request, a prefilled template, a commit sign-off or silence does
not constitute acceptance. Do not leave an uncompleted acceptance statement in
place: delete it if you are not accepting. Additional authors must each post their
own statement, unless a named representative provides documented authority for
their work. Do not publish identity documents, home addresses or employer contracts;
arrange a private contact route with a maintainer if confidential authority
evidence is needed.

Before merge, a maintainer records the accepting accounts, the agreement's base
commit and the contribution's final head commit in the pull request. The maintainer
confirms acceptance covers all included authors and any changes since acceptance;
request renewed confirmation if ownership, scope or the agreement version changes.
Retain that record when squashing commits. Changes to this agreement require a
new version and do not amend previously accepted grants retroactively. No external
signature service is required by this project procedure.
