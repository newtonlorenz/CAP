# Prepare a licence pack

**Licence Applications** is the normal starting point. A pack contains the main
application forms, repeated personal declarations, annexes and supporting documents
for one application. Existing annex-only and partial application records remain usable;
the checklist is a planning aid rather than a legal completeness determination.

## Set up the pack

Select the market and work through **Applicant and activities**, **Market questions**,
**Proposed contents**, then **Owners and access**. Review the checklist explicitly before
creation. Non-personal choices can be resumed in this browser session. Applicant details,
activity descriptions and personal names remain on the page until creation; re-enter them
after a refresh. A failed save preserves the current entries for retry.

Denmark proposes application 2-01, repeated Annex A 2-02 declarations, Annex B 2-03,
conditional representative Annex C 2-04 and supporting company/financial documents.
Confirm who needs a personal declaration against the
[Danish authority instructions](https://spillemyndigheden.dk/virksomheder-og-foreninger/spil-der-kraever-en-tilladelse/onlinekasino/soeg-en-tilladelse).

Finland distinguishes domestic and foreign applicants and responsible people without a
Finnish identity code. It proposes the relevant company, financial and conditional fitness
attachments. Use Finnish or Swedish for the actual application and check the current
[National Police Board instructions](https://poliisi.fi/en/applying-for-a-gambling-licence).
Licence preparation is separate from the audits required before operation. Denmark’s
certification guidance concerns licence issue. Sweden’s built-in checklist stays unpublished;
you can prepare a custom blank pack there.

Managers and organisation administrators maintain **Market setup**. Publishing a new
profile version affects future packs only. Each existing pack keeps its profile snapshot,
setup answers and checklist. Checklist changes require explicit review.

New packs default to **Highly confidential**. Sharing controls open after creation; add
people, select their permissions and save access. An assigned work owner does not
automatically receive access. Teams remain available from the workspace and sharing
controls. See [access control](access-control.md).

## Work through the pack

- **Overview:** stage, next action, applicant and access.
- **Forms and documents:** compact list, one editor at a time. Set up each form or
  annex, upload supporting documents and open forms to complete their answers.
- **Approval and submission:** readiness, internal review, frozen approval versions,
  recorded submissions and authority queries.
- **History:** the retained sequence of changes and decisions.

Create and attach a form in one save. Enter manual questions, use a saved blank form,
or import from Excel/CSV/TSV or reviewed requirement sources. Retain the original PDF or
other source document for reference. Imported questions require confirmation. Draft
requirement sources may be used for licence preparation with their status visible;
requirements approval is not a prerequisite.

Optional Jev checks suggest question mappings and missing metadata after explicit
external-processing confirmation. Normal import remains available without Jev. Review
inferred values before confirming. Saved questions retain requirement source references;
later source edits do not change the form.

A form supports up to 500 questions, grouped into sections. Supported answers include
text, multiline text, yes/no, choices, dates, numbers and evidence. Illustrative starters
remain available as reusable blank forms; they are not copies of official authority forms.
**Add another blank** copies an annex’s questions, without answers or attachments.
Answer reuse is a separate, explicit action with a retained source and fresh acceptance.

Answers save automatically. Failed saves retain drafts; conflicting edits pause autosave
for comparison with the latest saved answers. Acceptance is an explicit review action.
A meaningful answer change clears its acceptance. Keep the form open until saves finish.
Evidence must be accessible, current and intact for readiness. New attachments inherit
the work’s access controls when attached.

## Approve, submit and maintain

Form completion, acceptance, internal pack approval, recorded submission and an authority
decision are separate. Managers prepare and request review; approvers approve internally.
Contributors complete answers and respond to authority queries within their permissions.
Internal approval freezes an immutable version, including supporting file bytes.

Download the approved readable ZIP, submit through the authority’s channel, then record
the actual date and reference. CAP does not send applications. Start follow-up, record
queries, save responses and explicitly resolve them. Record the authority’s actual outcome
when the queries are resolved. Completion alone does not mean a licence was issued.

Return to draft with a reason to make corrections and obtain a new internal approval.
Earlier approved exports and submission records remain unchanged. Existing forms and
legacy links remain accessible through contextual links and **Existing forms**. Reusable
blank forms and evidence are secondary resources, not a required starting point.

Exports preserve reviewed CAP content in readable form. They do not reproduce or fill
the authority’s original PDF layout; original-layout authority form generation is a
separate follow-up. No automatic scheduling or new renewal tracking is introduced.

### Starting a pack

The applications list keeps existing work visible. Choose **New licence pack** to
open the creation view. **Cancel and return to applications** restores the list
and its filters. Safe draft choices can be resumed; applicant details and personal
names follow the existing privacy rules and must be re-entered after leaving.
